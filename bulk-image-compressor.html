How to Compress Multiple Images at Once

  1. Click the file picker and select your images, or drag them onto the box. You can add up to 20.
  2. Set the quality slider. It starts at 70%, a good balance for most photos.
  3. Click Compress all. Each image is processed in turn and you can watch the progress.
  4. Download images one by one, or click Download all as ZIP to get them together.

Good to Know

  • Each image keeps its format. JPG stays JPG, PNG stays PNG (transparency is preserved) and WebP stays WebP.
  • Resize in bulk. Enter a maximum width, such as 1200, and every image wider than that is scaled down in proportion. Smaller images are not enlarged.
  • One setting for the whole batch. If one image looks too soft, raise the quality and compress again.
  • Already optimized? If an image would not get smaller, your original is kept.
  • Your files stay private. Compression happens on your device, so nothing is sent to a server.
  • Large batches take longer. Big photos need more memory, so on a phone you may prefer smaller batches.
