Frequently asked questions

Will my photo look bad at 50 KB?

A 50 KB target is small, so large photos are usually scaled down as well as compressed. Small portraits and signatures stay clear; detailed full-size photos lose fine detail.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. Compression happens in your browser, so your images never leave your device.

Which formats are supported?

JPG, PNG and WebP. JPG stays JPG. PNG and WebP are saved as WebP to keep transparency, and in browsers that cannot create WebP they are saved as JPG with a white background.

What if my image is already smaller than the target?

The tool tells you and keeps your original file unchanged.
