Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

At ImgCompressor.shop, we respect your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it and the choices you have when you use our website.

Your Images

Image compression happens entirely in your web browser. The images you choose are processed on your device and are not uploaded to our servers, and we cannot see or store them. Compressed files are created on your device and saved only when you download them.

Information We Collect

You do not need an account to use ImgCompressor.shop. We may collect the following:

  • Usage data: through Google Analytics, such as pages visited, approximate location, device and browser type, and referring site. This data is aggregated and does not include your images.
  • Contact form data: if you write to us, we receive your name, email address and message. The form is processed by Web3Forms and delivered to us by email.
  • Preferences: your selected language is saved in your browser's local storage on your device only.

How We Use Information

We use this information to operate and improve the website, understand how it is used, keep it secure and reply to your messages. We do not sell your personal information.

Cookies and Local Storage

We and our partners may use cookies and similar technologies to analyze traffic, remember preferences and show advertising. Cookies are small files stored on your device. You can block or delete them in your browser settings, though some features may not work as well afterward.

Advertising

We may display ads through Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies, including the DoubleClick cookie, to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising in Google Ads Settings.

Third-Party Services

Our site uses third-party services that have their own privacy policies, including Google (Analytics and AdSense), Web3Forms (contact form) and Product Hunt (badge image shown in the footer). This policy does not apply to other websites or advertisers, and we encourage you to read their policies.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information we receive. Because your images stay on your device, they are never exposed to our servers.

Your Choices and Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you, or to object to certain processing. To make a request, such as deleting a message you sent us, use our contact page.

Children's Information

ImgCompressor.shop does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided such information, please contact us and we will delete it.

User Consent

By using this website, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

Updates to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please visit our contact page.
